Ask a question
What exactly is wall printing?

We print the picture you choose directly onto your painted wall, the way a printer prints on paper, only much bigger. There's no wallpaper or sticker, so there are no edges to peel. The picture becomes part of your wall.

Do you print for offices and commercial spaces?

Yes. Along with homes, we print walls for corporate offices, reception areas, shops, cafés, restaurants, hotels, hospitals, schools and apartment common areas such as lobbies, clubhouses and corridors. Send the wall sizes and your brief or logo on WhatsApp and we'll share a preview and price.

How is the price decided?

It depends on the size of the area you'd like printed and the design. The bigger the wall, the less you pay per sq ft. Send your wall size and a photo on WhatsApp and we'll reply with an exact price and a preview, usually the same day. No visit needed for a quote.

Is wall printing better than wallpaper?

For a single feature wall, usually yes. A printed wall has no seams or edges, so nothing lifts in Bengaluru's monsoon humidity, and you can use any photo or artwork in full detail. Wallpaper is a good choice for repeating patterns across many walls, or for rented homes where you need to remove it.

How long does it take?

Most walls are printed in a few hours. The colours are dry the moment they're printed, so you can use the room the same evening. For very large walls, we'll confirm the timing with your quote.

Can I clean it?

Yes. The print is waterproof and scratch proof, so you can wipe it with a soft, damp cloth. It handles everyday life, including curious little hands. Just avoid harsh scrubbers and chemicals.

How long will it last?

Indoors, a printed wall stays bright for 12 years or more. If a wall gets strong direct sunlight, we'll talk you through the best spot.

Can I use my own photo or artwork?

Of course. Family photos, your child's drawing, a painting you love or your company logo. Send it on WhatsApp and we'll tell you how big it can go while still looking sharp.

Does the wall need to be painted first?

Usually not. If your paint is smooth and clean, we print right on it. If there's damp, cracks or peeling paint, those need fixing first so the picture lasts.

How much space do you need in front of the wall?

About 4 ft of free space in front of the wall, so we can set up the printer. Please move furniture away before we arrive. We'll also need a power point nearby, and a lift if the wall is on the 1st floor or above.

Is it safe for children's rooms?

Yes. There's no wet paint and hardly any smell, so nurseries and kids' rooms can be used the same day.

What else can you print on?

Painted walls are ideal. We can also print on wood, glass, metal and tiles. For these, we try a small patch first to make sure you'll love the result.

Can I change the design later?

Yes. When you're ready for something new, the wall can be painted over like any other wall, or we can print a new design.

Do you come outside Bengaluru?

We cover all of Bengaluru. For sites outside the city, we add a travel charge based on the distance. Message us your town and wall size.
